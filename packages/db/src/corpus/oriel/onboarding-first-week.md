# Onboarding: the first week

Welcome. The first week at Oriel is built so that by Friday you have met your team, understood what a charging hub is, shipped one small thing and know who to ask. Your manager and your buddy own this plan; People Ops checks on Wednesday that it is on track.

## Before day one

People Ops sends the contract, the IT form and the handbook link ten days before the start. IT ships the laptop to the office or to the home address for remote starters, set up and encrypted, with the password in a separate message. Your manager writes a one-page note on your first project and names a buddy from another team.

## Day one, Monday

09:30 at the Paris office (or the Lyon depot for field roles): badge, laptop, accounts. 10:30 the welcome session with People Ops: who is who, the handbook in twenty minutes, how pay and leave work. Lunch with the team. 14:00 with your manager: the first project, the first-week plan, how one-on-ones work. 16:00 with your buddy: the tools, the chat channels that matter, where the coffee is.

## Day two, Tuesday

The product day. The morning is the hub walkthrough with a field technician: what a hub is, how a courier badges in, what the telemetry shows, what goes wrong. In the afternoon you read the three runbooks (on-call, deploying, backups) and the support SLA page, and you shadow an hour of support with the duty agent.

## Day three, Wednesday

Your first change. Engineers pick a starter ticket from the "first week" column and ship it with a reviewer by the end of the day; operations people run one full support case with their buddy; sales and account people join a customer call as an observer. People Ops checks in at 15:00 for fifteen minutes.

## Day four, Thursday

The company meeting, every other Thursday at 11:00, where the week's numbers and the hub map are reviewed. On the other weeks it is the team's planning meeting. The afternoon is the security training (forty minutes, online, with a quiz you need to pass before you get access to customer data).

## Day five, Friday

A written note to your manager: what you understood, what surprised you, what you would change. It is read, not graded. Then the Friday demo at 16:00, where you can show the starter ticket.

## The first three months

Weekly one-on-ones with your manager; a thirty-day check with People Ops; a written review at the end of the trial period (two months for most roles, four for managers). The buddy stays yours for three months. The goal of the first quarter is one project you own end to end.

## Who to ask

Accounts and access: IT, in the portal. Pay, leave and the contract: People Ops. Anything about a hub: the duty technician, on the field channel. Anything about a customer: the account owner in the CRM. If you do not know who, ask your buddy.
