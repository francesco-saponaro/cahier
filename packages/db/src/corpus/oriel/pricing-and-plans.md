# Pricing and plans

Oriel sells access to its charging hubs to delivery companies, not to individual couriers. The price is per badge per month, with a usage component for charging. This page is the public price list as of this year and the rules sales people apply; discounts outside these rules go through the commercial lead.

## Plans

Starter: up to 20 badges, 19 euros per badge per month, access to the hubs in one city, charging billed at 0.45 euros per kWh, support by email within one working day. Fleet: from 21 badges, 15 euros per badge per month, all cities, charging at 0.39 euros per kWh, a named account owner, support within four hours in working hours. Network: from 200 badges, priced per contract, with reserved slots at chosen hubs, charging at 0.35 euros per kWh, support within one hour around the clock, and quarterly reviews.

## What a badge is

A badge is one active courier credential. A badge is billed for every month in which it is active on at least one day. Deactivating a badge on the 3rd and reactivating it on the 25th bills one badge for that month, not two. Badges can be moved between couriers; the data of the previous courier is deleted 30 days after the move.

## Charging

Charging is metered per session from the slot's meter and billed monthly in arrears at the plan's rate, rounded to the cent per session. Sessions shorter than two minutes are not billed, because they are almost always a courier plugging in the wrong slot. A session interrupted by a hub fault is not billed, and the customer sees it as "waived" on the invoice.

## Reserved slots

Network customers can reserve slots at a hub: a reserved slot is held for the customer's badges between the hours they choose and costs 60 euros per slot per month. A reserved slot that was not used at all in a month is credited back at half price, once, as a reminder that it is probably not needed.

## Discounts

Annual payment in advance: 10 % off the badge fee. A second city for a Starter customer: the plan moves to Fleet. Non-profit delivery cooperatives: 20 % off the badge fee on any plan, on presentation of the statutes. No discounts on charging, ever, because the electricity is a cost passed through at a thin margin.

## Billing

Invoices are issued on the first working day of the month for the previous month, due in 30 days. Payment is by direct debit on Starter, and by transfer or direct debit on the other plans. A customer 15 days late gets a reminder; at 45 days the badges are suspended with a week's notice to the account owner; at 60 days the contract is handed to finance.

## Trials

A new customer can run a two-week trial with up to five badges in one city, free, including charging up to 50 kWh. The trial ends with a call with the account owner. Trials are not extended; a customer who needs more time signs a monthly Starter plan instead.
