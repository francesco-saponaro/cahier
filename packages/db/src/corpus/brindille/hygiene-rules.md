# Hygiene rules

Brindille follows the French food hygiene regulation, and the inspector can come at any hour. The rules below are the ones that matter every day; the full plan is in the binder behind the counter.

## People

Hands are washed on arrival, after the toilet, after handling money, after touching the bin and every time you switch from raw to baked. Hair is covered in the bakehouse. Jewellery stays at home except a plain ring. Anyone with a stomach illness stays at home for 48 hours after the last symptom and tells the manager.

## Temperatures

Fridges are checked and written down twice a day, at opening and at 15:00. Hot products are kept above 63 degrees or sold within two hours of baking. Cream products are made the same day and thrown away at closing, no exception.

## Cleaning

The bakehouse floor is washed every evening and the tables after every batch. The display is emptied and wiped every evening. The deep clean of the oven and the extraction is on Sunday evening, logged in the binder with the name of who did it.

## Waste

Unsold bread goes in the collection bags for the charity at 19:30. Everything else goes in the bins, sorted. Nothing is ever taken home and sold.
