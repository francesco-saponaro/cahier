# Staffing rota rules

A bakery runs on its rota. The rota is written two weeks ahead by the manager, posted on the wall and in the app on the Thursday, and changed after that only by swaps agreed by both people and the manager.

## Shifts

Opening baker 04:30 to 12:00. Second baker 06:00 to 13:30. Opening sales 06:15 to 14:00. Afternoon sales 13:30 to 19:45. Closing baker, on Fridays and Saturdays only, 12:00 to 19:30. Everyone gets a 30-minute break, paid, after four hours.

## Days

Nobody works more than six days in a row or more than two Sundays a month. A full-time week is 35 hours; hours above 35 in a week are overtime, paid at the legal rate, and need the manager's agreement before, not after. Public holidays are open except 1 May and 25 December, and a public holiday worked is paid double.

## Swaps and absences

A swap is written in the app by the person giving the shift and accepted by the person taking it; the manager approves within a day. Sickness is reported to the manager by phone before 05:00 for a morning shift and before 11:00 for an afternoon shift. Being late three times in a month means a conversation with the manager.

## Students and seasonal staff

Student contracts are for Saturdays and the school holidays, at most 20 hours a week during term. Summer and Christmas seasonal staff are hired for six weeks and trained on the opening checklist on their first day.
