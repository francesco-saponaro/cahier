# Support SLAs

Support at Oriel is the duty agent during the day and the on-call engineer at night. The promises below are in the customer contracts, so missing them is a contractual matter, not just a bad day. The support tool measures all of them automatically.

## Hours

Working hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 19:00 Paris time, excluding French public holidays. Network customers have support around the clock, through the on-call phone line for anything that stops couriers.

## Response times

The response time is the time to the first human answer, not to the fix. Starter: one working day. Fleet: four working hours. Network: one hour, any time. An automatic acknowledgement does not count as an answer.

## Resolution targets

A courier blocked at a hub: two hours to a workaround on every plan, because the fallback is always to open the slot remotely or send the technician. A hub fully down: four hours to restore or to redirect the customer's badges to the nearest hub, with the charging difference waived. A billing question: five working days. A feature request: an answer within ten working days saying whether and when, never silence.

## Priorities

P1: couriers cannot badge in or charge. P2: one feature broken, a workaround exists. P3: a question or a small defect. P4: an idea. The customer suggests the priority and the duty agent confirms it; disagreements go to the account owner. A P1 that lasts more than four hours is also an incident for the engineering team.

## Credits

If a Fleet or Network customer's hubs were unavailable for more than 0.5 % of the month (about three and a half hours), the badge fee for that month is credited by 10 %; above 2 %, by 25 %; above 5 %, by 50 %. Availability is measured per hub from the fleet service's view, excluding announced maintenance windows and site power cuts. Credits are applied on the next invoice without the customer asking.

## Maintenance windows

Planned maintenance is announced at least five working days ahead on the status page and by email to the account owners, happens between 01:00 and 05:00, and never on the same hub two weeks in a row.

## Escalation

A customer who is not satisfied with an answer can ask for the account owner; the account owner can bring in the head of operations. Every escalation is logged with what was promised. Twice a year the account owners read the escalations together and change the rules on this page if a pattern shows.

## What support does not do

Repair a courier's bike, give legal advice on employment, or share another customer's data, even when the two customers share a hub.
