# Expenses and travel

Oriel pays back the money you spend for work. The rule of thumb: if you would be embarrassed to explain a receipt to a colleague, do not submit it. Claims go in the expenses tool with a photo of the receipt within 30 days of the spend; claims older than 60 days are refused.

## What is covered

Travel between the Paris and Lyon offices by train in second class, booked through the travel tool so the ticket is paid by the company directly. Taxis and ride-hailing only when public transport is not reasonable: before 06:30, after 22:30, with heavy equipment, or in a city where there is no transit line to the site. Meals on a day with travel are covered up to 25 euros for lunch and 35 euros for dinner, including drinks; a receipt is required above 10 euros. A hotel night is covered up to 140 euros in Paris and 110 euros elsewhere in France, breakfast included. Trips abroad are covered at the real cost, booked in advance through the travel tool.

## What is not covered

Alcohol outside a meal, fines, upgrades of any kind, personal insurance, and anything bought for a personal purpose. A meal shared with people from outside Oriel is a client meal and is approved by a department head before the event, not after.

## Site visits

Most travel at Oriel is to a hub site: a car park, a depot or a street where a charging hub is installed. For a site visit, the technician on duty books the trip, and the hub reference goes in the expense claim so the cost lands on the right project. Safety boots and a high-visibility jacket are provided by the company and are not an expense.

## Company cards

Team leads and the field technicians hold a company card for purchases under 500 euros. Every card payment still needs a receipt in the tool within 30 days. A card payment with no receipt after 60 days is treated as a personal advance and deducted from the next payslip, after a reminder.

## Equipment

Laptops, screens, keyboards and phones are ordered by IT from the hardware catalogue, never bought on expenses. Small items for home work (a cable, a stand) are allowed up to 60 euros a year on expenses with a receipt.

## Mileage

Using your own car for work is allowed only when there is no train and no company vehicle. Mileage is paid at the official French rate for your car's fiscal power, and the claim must say the start, the end and the reason for the trip. Parking at the destination is covered; parking at the office is not.

## Approval

Claims under 100 euros are approved automatically unless the tool flags them. Claims between 100 and 1,000 euros are approved by your manager; above 1,000 euros by the finance lead. Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run, on the 28th of the month, or on the 15th for claims approved before the 10th.
