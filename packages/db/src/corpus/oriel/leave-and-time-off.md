# Leave and time off

This page is the rule book for days away from work at Oriel. It applies to everyone on a permanent contract in France; interns and people on a fixed-term contract get the same days pro rata. If something here contradicts your contract, the contract wins, and please tell People Ops so the page gets fixed.

## Paid leave

Everyone earns 25 working days of paid leave a year, counted from 1 June to 31 May, which is the French reference period. Days are earned at 2.08 per month worked and can be taken as soon as they are earned. Up to five days can be carried into the next period; anything above five is lost on 31 May unless a manager has approved an exception in writing before 30 April.

Leave is requested in the HR tool at least two weeks ahead for one to four days, and at least six weeks ahead for a week or more. Your manager answers within five working days. A request that gets no answer in that time is approved. Between 20 December and 2 January the office runs with a skeleton team, so leave in that window is approved by rota, first come first served, with at most two people away per team.

## Public holidays

The eleven French public holidays are days off for everyone, whichever office you work from. When a holiday falls on a weekend it is not moved. The Lyon office closes for Pentecost Monday; the Paris office treats it as the solidarity day and stays open, which is why Lyon people see one fewer working day in May.

## Sick leave

Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day, by message or phone, and send the medical certificate to People Ops within 48 hours. Oriel tops up the social security payment to full salary from the first day for up to 90 days a year; after that the collective agreement's rates apply. Nobody is asked to work while on sick leave, and a manager who messages someone on sick leave about work will hear from People Ops.

## Family leave

Parental leave follows the legal scheme: 16 weeks of maternity leave, 25 days of paternity and second-parent leave to be taken within six months of the birth, both paid at full salary. Oriel adds four weeks of paid leave to either, to be taken in the first year. Adoption leave gets the same addition. Carer's leave for a close relative is five paid days a year, plus unpaid leave by agreement.

## Other absences

Moving house is two paid days once every three years. Jury duty and the citizens' reserve are paid at full salary on presentation of the summons. Unpaid leave of up to three months can be requested after two years of service and is decided by the department head within a month.

## Working from another country

You can work up to 20 days a year from another EU country without a formal request, as long as your manager knows and you keep French working hours. Anything longer, or any country outside the EU, needs a written agreement from People Ops at least a month ahead, because of tax and insurance.
