# Remote work and equipment

Oriel is an office-first company with real flexibility. Most teams are in the office three days a week, with Tuesday and Thursday as the days everyone in a team is in. The other days are yours to choose, within the rules below.

## Where you can work

From home, from either office, or from a coworking space the company has an agreement with (the list is in the IT portal). Cafés and trains are fine for reading and messages but not for calls about customers or for anything with hub telemetry on the screen. Field technicians work from the sites and from the Lyon depot; their office days are the Monday planning meeting and the monthly safety review.

## Hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 Paris time, when everyone is reachable. Outside core hours you set your own day within the legal limits: no more than 10 hours a day, 48 hours a week, and at least 11 hours of rest between two days. The right to disconnect is real: nobody has to read messages after 19:00 or before 08:00, and the chat tool is set to deliver scheduled messages at 08:30 by default.

## Equipment

Everyone gets a laptop from the catalogue (a 14-inch model by default, 16-inch on request for people who build hub firmware or run the data pipelines), a screen for home, a headset, and a phone if the role needs one. Equipment stays the company's and comes back when you leave. A broken laptop is replaced within two working days from the Paris stock; report it to IT in the portal, not by message to a colleague.

## Home office allowance

People who work from home at least two days a week get 30 euros a month, paid with the salary, for electricity and internet. It needs no receipt. A one-off budget of 300 euros for a chair and a desk is available in the first six months, on expenses with a receipt.

## Security at home

Work on the company laptop only, with the disk encrypted (it is by default) and the screen locked when you step away. Home Wi-Fi must have a password; a public Wi-Fi is used with the VPN on. Printing customer data at home is not allowed. Family members do not use the work laptop, even for a quick search.

## Meetings

A meeting with one remote person is a remote meeting: everyone joins from their own laptop with the camera on, even in the office, so nobody is half in the room. Meetings are 25 or 50 minutes long so the next one can start on time. Every recurring meeting has an owner who cancels it when there is nothing to discuss.

## Leaving the company

On the last day the laptop, the badge and the phone go back to IT, and the accounts are closed the same evening. The home screen and headset can be kept for a token price of 20 euros if you have been at Oriel more than a year.
