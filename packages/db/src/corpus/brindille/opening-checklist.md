# Opening checklist

Every Brindille bakery opens at 07:00, and the first customer at 07:01 should find bread on the shelf, a clean floor and a smiling person at the till. This checklist is what the opening baker and the opening sales person do, in order, from 04:30.

## The baker, from 04:30

Switch on the oven first, before anything else, so it reaches 240 degrees by 05:30. Check the fridge temperatures and write them on the sheet: the walk-in at 3 degrees or below, the display fridge at 4 or below. A fridge above 5 degrees means the food inside is checked piece by piece and the manager is called before 06:00. Take the dough out of the retarder in the order of the plan: baguettes first, then the loaves, then the viennoiseries. First bake in the oven at 05:45, second at 06:30, third at 07:15 so the shelf is full when the school rush starts at 08:00.

## The sales person, from 06:15

Wipe the counter and the display, put out the price tags, check the till float of 150 euros, and switch on the card terminal. Put the pastries in the display from the left, the bread on the shelves behind, the sandwiches in the cold display with the dates on them. Unlock the door at 06:58 and switch the sign.

## Both

A quick look at the sales board for the day: what was left yesterday, what is ordered for today, which special is on. If a delivery of flour or butter comes in the morning, the baker signs for it and checks the temperature of the butter at the door; butter above 8 degrees goes back on the truck.

## Allergens

Every product on the shelf has its allergen card, and the card is checked on Monday against the recipe book. A customer who asks about an ingredient gets the card, not a guess.
